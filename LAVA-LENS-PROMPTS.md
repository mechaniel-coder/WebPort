## GLASSES QC — run on every still before you animate

1. **One continuous shield lens**, not two separate lenses
2. **Scalloped notch** cut at the nose bridge
3. Thin chrome line along the top brow
4. Lava gradient: red-purple edges into orange-amber center
5. Gloss black frame, flat side-shield panels

Miss on 1 or 2 → regenerate, those are the silhouette. Miss on 3–5 → one retry,
then move on; at action scale nobody is checking.

## FRAMING QC — the most common wasted generation

Each still is deliberately the moment *before* the payoff, because the video
animates forward from it. If your still already shows the peak, regenerate.

| Still | Must NOT be in frame yet |
|---|---|
| 2A | Standing — he's still prone, the pop-up is the animation |
| 3A | The spray fan — the rail is only just entering the water |
| 5A | Trucks landed — he's still airborne, the grind is the animation |
| 6A | Any descent — he's balanced on the coping |
| 7A | Takeoff — he's still on the ramp face, compressed |

## EDIT NOTES

- **The 180 transition** is built in post. Clip 4 ends in a right whip; Clip 5
  opens clean on the skatepark. Put a directional motion-blur transition between
  them in your editor and cut on the blur.
- **Trim every clip to 5–7 seconds.** The last third of a 10s generation is where
  motion drifts and faces melt.
- **Generate 3–4 takes per clip.** Roughly one in three comes back with clean
  physics. That ratio is normal.
- **Single grade pass across all eight** at the end. Clips drift in color
  temperature even from identical prompts, and the grade is what makes it read as
  one spot instead of eight generations.
